  - Repro / evidence: as Ivy, resumed `eng_devices` mid-way (hub's own "Pick up where you left off … step 3 of 3" card — a genuine cross-session Practice resume, which DOES work), finished Practice 3/3, entered Mastery, answered mastery question 1 wrong then correct (AI "Why isn't that right?" explanation shown, checker-gated, worked as designed), advanced to mastery question 2 of 3 (the exact seeded "Which sentence best explains the EFFECT of the metaphor…" item). At that point I simulated a page refresh (`browser_navigate` to the identical lesson URL, equivalent to hitting reload). Instead of resuming at "Mastery check 1: 2 of 3" with the score intact, the lesson **reset all the way to the Explainer** ("Today's lesson · Language Devices" with "I'm ready — let's practise") — full practice AND the mastery-question-1 pass I'd just completed were gone, with no warning or acknowledgement that anything had been lost.
  - Root cause: `lessonPhase` (`practice_player.tsx:266-268`) always initialises to `"practice"` on every fresh mount — there is no persisted signal anywhere that a child had progressed into `"mastery"`/`"reteach"`/`"handoff"`. The resume-on-mount effect (line 575) is explicitly gated `lessonPhase !== "practice"` → bail, and `persist()` (the function that actually writes `{step, score, total}` to `lesson_progress` + localStorage) is called **only** `else if (lessonPhase === "practice")` (line 1322-1323) — never once during Mastery, Reteach, or Handoff. The moment Practice finishes, `clearProgress()` wipes the one saved practice checkpoint (line 1314) and the lesson moves on with zero further persistence for the rest of the session. Nothing here is `isReview`-specific — the same mechanism applies identically to a **first-time** certification attempt, so any child whose tablet reloads, browser crashes, or who taps back mid-Mastery loses the entire lesson, not just the mastery portion. This directly contradicts the documented architecture promise in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:192` ("one row per child per topic … so an interrupted child resumes at the exact step").
  - Fix: extend the persisted checkpoint to carry the sub-phase, not just a step/score pair scoped to the practice array. Concretely: add an optional `phase: "practice" | "mastery"` (default `"practice"` for legacy rows) plus `masteryAttempt` to `SavedProgress`/`LessonProgressDoc`; call the equivalent of today's `persist()` after each answered mastery question (mirroring the existing practice call site at line 1322-1324, using `masterySet.length` as the mastery-phase total); on mount, `resolveResumeStep`'s mastery-aware sibling should route `lessonPhase` to `"mastery"` at the saved step/attempt instead of falling through to `"explainer"` when a valid mastery checkpoint exists. Reteach/Handoff can reasonably remain non-resumable (they're short, AI-mediated, or already terminal states) as long as landing back in a fresh Mastery attempt 1 is calm rather than a full Explainer replay — worth a product decision, but even "restart Mastery attempt 1 with a warm explanatory line" is a materially better landing than today's silent full reset.
  - Risk: Medium — touches the resume architecture, a core, already-unit-tested pure engine (`resolveResumeStep`/`clampResumeScore`), and the mastery-attempt/reteach state machine. New unit tests should assert: a saved mastery checkpoint resumes into `"mastery"` at the right step/attempt with score intact; a stale/mismatched-total mastery save still falls back to a fresh start (mirroring the existing "content changed" guard); reteach/handoff still degrade calmly rather than crashing.
