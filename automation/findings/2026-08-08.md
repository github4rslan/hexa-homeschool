## Bugs (Critical → Low) — batch 2

- [x] **B3 · Medium · Ambiguous/likely-wrong canonical answer: "where is most water absorbed in digestion?"** — `src/lib/data/curriculum.seed.extra.ts:139` (`sci_body`, diagnostic)
  - Question (verbatim): prompt "Where is most water absorbed in digestion?",
    options `["stomach", "large intestine", "mouth", "oesophagus"]`,
    `correct_index: 1` ("large intestine"), explanation "The large intestine
    absorbs most remaining water."
  - Why it is a problem: at GCSE/A-level physiology the **small intestine**
    absorbs the large majority of water (roughly 80 percent of the daily water
    load), and the large intestine only reabsorbs the smaller remainder. The word
    "most" makes the keyed answer defensible only under the simplified
    primary-school framing "the large intestine's job is to absorb water", which
    contradicts the literal "most". A child who has learned the correct GCSE fact
    (the small intestine absorbs most of the water and the digested food) would be
    marked wrong. This is exactly the "a second option is also defensible / the
    canonical answer is arguably wrong" case the authoring rules say to flag, not
    silently teach.
  - Recommended fix (human confirms first, Mechanic then edits the seed;
    read-and-flag only, I did NOT edit it): the ambiguity is in the prompt itself,
    so the safest route is to RETIRE this item and replace it with an unambiguous
    one, e.g. prompt "Which organ's main job is to reabsorb water from the
    material left after digestion?" options `["stomach","large intestine","mouth","liver"]`
    correct_index 1, explanation "The large intestine (colon) reabsorbs water from
    the material left after the small intestine has absorbed the digested food."
    That phrasing is unambiguous and still tests the intended fact. (Note: the seed
    upserts on `topic_tag + prompt`, so changing the prompt inserts a new row and
    orphans the old one, per the memory note, so retire-and-replace is the clean
    path here, not an in-place prompt edit.)
  - Risk: none technical; this is a content-truth call for a human to confirm
    before Mechanic touches the seed. No other seed answer needs review (I checked
    all of them).
