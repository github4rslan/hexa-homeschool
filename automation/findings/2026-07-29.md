## Bugs (Critical → Low)

- [x] **B1 · Low · Science questions show a decorative/generic figure where maths shows a precise derived one** — `src/components/child/practice-player.tsx:1525-1552`, `src/lib/child/math-visual.ts`
  - Evidence: the always-present question figure only renders a real diagram when `deriveMathVisual(prompt)` matches — and that deriver is maths-only (`fraction | percent | number_line | array | groups | algebra_tiles`). For a science prompt it falls to the AI PNG branch, which is rendered `alt=""` `role="presentation"` (correct, since its alt is a fixed template). Net effect: a **sighted** child on a science question sees a generic AI image with no guaranteed tie to the concept, while a **screen-reader / auto-narration** child gets *no* figure at all — an inconsistency with maths, where the figure is exact and honestly described.
  - Root cause: no science branch in the derived-visual system; science leans entirely on the optional AI image.
  - Fix: the smallest correct fix is F2 (add a science deriver). If shipped without F2, at minimum keep the current decorative behaviour (do not regress alt).
  - Risk: none for the alt behaviour; see F2 for the additive path.
