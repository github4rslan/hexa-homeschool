## Bugs (Critical → Low)

- [ ] **B1 · High · `TOTAL_TOPICS`/`PORTFOLIO_TOTAL_TOPICS` are hardcoded to the ORIGINAL "10 per subject" curriculum size and no longer match the real topic count, corrupting both the dashboard progress display AND the compliance-portfolio "complete" status** — `src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/page.tsx:61`, `src/app/api/portfolio/route.ts:20-21`, `src/lib/db/repo.ts:1686-1712` (`countCertified`, `certifiedBySubject`)
  - Repro / evidence: as the SMOKE parent, dashboard shows Ivy's child card reading **"Curriculum mastery 31 / 30"** — a >100% progress bar (visually overflowing its container, `width: 103.3%`) — and her own child-profile page independently confirms "**31 topics certified so far**". Read the code to root-cause rather than guessing: `TOTAL_TOPICS = 30 // seeded curriculum size (10 per subject × 3)` (`dashboard/page.tsx:61`) is a STALE comment/constant — the real GCSE-band curriculum in `curriculum.seed.ts` now has **14 maths + 10 english + 10 science = 34** topics (EPIC 3's `maths_inequalities`/`maths_transformations`/`maths_simultaneous` additions took maths from 10 to 14 topics, confirmed by counting `BASE_SEED_TOPICS` entries directly), and there's a SEPARATE `curriculum.seed.bands.ts` with **12 more** KS2/KS3 pre-GCSE band topics (Arithmetic, Fractions & Measures, Negative Numbers & Powers, Algebraic Expressions, Reading & Spelling, Sentences & Punctuation, Grammar & Clauses, Living Things, Materials & Their Properties, Forces & Energy, Cells & Organisms, Inference & Language — all visible as real certificates in Ivy's list) — **46 real topics total**, not 30. `countCertified()`/`certifiedBySubject()` (`repo.ts:1686`, `:1692`) correctly count `state: "certified"` docs across ALL bands with no filter, so the numerator has always been able to exceed the stale denominator once a child works through both a pre-GCSE band and its GCSE band in the same subject — exactly Ivy's real, long-lived progression, not an edge case.
  - This is not just a display glitch — it corrupts a **compliance-facing** number too: `api/portfolio/route.ts`'s `PORTFOLIO_TOTAL_TOPICS = 30` and `PORTFOLIO_TOPICS_PER_SUBJECT = 10` clamp each subject's certified count to 10 before summing (`Math.min(certified[subject], 10)`), so a subject where a child has certified, say, 6 GCSE topics + 4 pre-GCSE band topics (10 total, hitting the clamp) is reported to the Local Authority as **"10/10 topics, complete"** even though the real GCSE-only topic count for that subject is higher (14 for maths) and several real GCSE topics remain uncertified. Combined with `mocksTaken >= totalMocks`, this can flip the whole portfolio's `status` to `"complete"` while GCSE coverage is genuinely partial — the exact kind of overstated-readiness number a parent or LA officer would reasonably rely on.
  - Root cause: two independent hardcoded "30"/"10" constants that were never revisited as EPIC 2/3 grew the curriculum, plus no distinction anywhere in the counting logic between a "pre-GCSE prerequisite" band topic and a genuine GCSE-spec topic — both currently count identically toward "curriculum mastery" and toward the compliance total.
  - Fix: replace both hardcoded constants with a live count derived from the topics collection/seed data, scoped to `key_stage: 4` (GCSE) topics only per subject (e.g. a new `countTopicsBySubject(keyStage = 4)` in `repo.ts`, or a simple `SEED_TOPICS.filter(t => t.subject === s && t.key_stage === 4).length` if a static count is acceptable given topics only change via a deploy). Then: (1) `dashboard/page.tsx` computes `TOTAL_TOPICS` as the sum across subjects instead of a literal `30`; (2) `portfolio/route.ts` computes `PORTFOLIO_TOPICS_PER_SUBJECT` per-subject dynamically instead of a flat `10`, and drops the pre-GCSE band topics from the compliance total entirely (or, if the owner wants band-topic progress to still show somewhere, surface it as a clearly-separate "foundations" line rather than folding it into the GCSE completeness percentage). Add a unit test asserting the portfolio `status` never reports `"complete"` while any real GCSE topic for that child's subjects remains uncertified.
  - Risk: Low-medium — pure counting-logic change, no schema change; the portfolio status change is the more sensitive edge (re-verify against a child who is genuinely fully GCSE-certified, once one exists, that status still correctly reads "complete"). Repro confirmed via two independent page reads (dashboard card + child-profile page agreeing on the same "31") rather than a screenshot.
